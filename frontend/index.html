Manganese Prospectivity Mapping

AI/ML + Satellite Remote Sensing for Manganese Exploration

1. Project Overview

Using AI/ML and Space Technology to Identify Manganese Reserves and Overcome Production Shortfalls. The system learns from real GSI manganese occurrences plus Sentinel-2 and SRTM terrain data and outputs predicted prospectivity. It does not confirm reserves: that needs field investigation and official geological assessment. By ranking zones, it can narrow exploration search areas and help plan field surveys; it does not itself raise mining output.
